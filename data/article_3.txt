A leaked internal memo obtained by TechLeak suggests the NovaCell 4 will not ship before late 2027, several months later than Nova Dynamics has publicly stated. The memo, attributed to an unnamed production manager, describes ongoing yield problems at the pilot facility in Arizona and puts the realistic energy density closer to 360 watt-hours per kilogram once manufacturing tolerances are accounted for. Nova Dynamics has not responded to requests for comment on the memo. TechLeak has a mixed track record on prior leaks, correctly predicting two product delays in 2023 but incorrectly reporting a canceled product line in 2022.
