Industry analysts at BatteryWatch confirmed that Nova Dynamics has pushed the NovaCell 4 launch to March 2027, citing sources close to the company's testing division. The energy density figure of roughly 420 Wh/kg was independently verified by a third-party lab in Munich, which called the result credible but noted that mass production yields at that density remain unproven. BatteryWatch's report also flagged that Nova Dynamics has missed two previous launch targets, in 2024 and 2025, raising questions about whether the March 2027 date will hold either.
