Nova Dynamics announced today that its new solid-state battery, the NovaCell 4, will begin shipping to automakers in March 2027. Company representatives said the battery achieves an energy density of 420 watt-hours per kilogram, a figure that would make it one of the densest commercially available cells on the market. Chief engineer Maria Solano said the delay from the originally planned 2026 launch was due to thermal stability issues discovered during cold-weather testing in Norway. Two automotive partners, listed only as "a major European manufacturer" and "a North American electric truck startup," have signed early supply agreements. Nova Dynamics stock rose 6 percent following the announcement.
